About

A quantum researcher and engineer at heart

GM

I am a quantum technologies researcher and licensed Electrical Engineer with a PhD from the University of Naples, Federico II, and the National Institute of Optics (CNR-INO) in Florence, Italy. My work focuses on experimental quantum optics, quantum communication, and quantum photonics.

Currently, I am a Government of Ireland Postdoctoral Researcher at Tyndall National Institute, University College Cork, where I work on cavity-integrated molecular photon interfaces for scalable photonic graph state generation.

My research journey has taken me across four countries — Pakistan, China, Italy, and Ireland — where I have developed expertise in experimental quantum optics, quantum key distribution (QKD), single-photon sources, quantum satellite communications, and optical communication systems. I am passionate about advancing secure quantum technologies and contributing to teaching, research supervision, and curriculum development.
